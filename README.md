Dust storms pose major environmental and health risks across South Asia, influencing air 
quality, visibility, and land degradation. This study applies satellite observations and simple 
machine-learning techniques to map dust occurrence and estimate dust risk across India 
and Sri Lanka. By combining MODIS Aerosol Optical Depth (AOD), vegetation indices, 
and climate variables from TerraClimate, a logistic regression model was developed to 
classify dust events and generate a monthly and annual dust-risk map for the region.
